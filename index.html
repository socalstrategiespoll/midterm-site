Race margins are two-party, Republican minus Democratic. VoteHub win probability is the chance the Republican wins. Polymarket prices are in cents for a Yes share on each party, read live from Polymarket in your browser.

The chamber bars count every seat. Races we model use our baseline margin, and every other seat goes to whichever party VoteHub gives the better chance. Senate seats and governorships not on the ballot this year count for the party that holds them. The paler part of each bar is those safe or off-year seats.

Edge is the gap between the VoteHub probability and what you would pay on Polymarket, using the ask on whichever side is cheaper against VoteHub. A big edge can mean the market knows something VoteHub does not, and thin markets can show gaps that nobody can actually trade, so treat it as a place to look.
