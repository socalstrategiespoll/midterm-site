DemRep
Pick a race on the map or from the list.

Race margins are two-party, Republican minus Democratic. Win probability is the chance the Republican wins, as published by VoteHub. Kalshi prices are in cents on the same side.

Edge is the gap between the VoteHub probability and the price you would actually pay, using the ask to buy the Republican or the bid to buy the Democrat. A big edge can mean the market knows something VoteHub does not, so treat it as a place to look.
